Join the Team

Would you like to work with us? Do not hesitate to contact me to learn about future research opportunities.
I do my best to respond to every email, but I may not if your request is too generic or if you do not provide enough information about your motivation and qualifications for the opportunity you are enquiring about.

Undergraduate researchers

If you are an undergraduate student interested in working with our group, feel free to send me an email with a CV, unofficial transcript, and a brief statement about your interests and motivations.

Each year, Saint Mary's University is sponsoring several full-time summer research positions. The application deadline is usually in February. You are welcome to reach out ahead of the deadline to discuss potential projects.

Students who want to apply for the Honours in Astrophysics program are welcome to contact me to discuss potential research thesis projects for the following academic year.

Graduate researchers

I am planning to recruit new graduate students (MSc or PhD in Astronomy) for a start in Fall 2027.

Refer to the Faculty of Graduate Studies and Research for information on how to apply to our graduate program. The application deadline is February 1st for a start in September the same year.

You are welcome to email me to let me know that you plan to apply and to discuss potential research projects. Be sure to include a CV, unofficial transcript, information on your skills, experience, coursework and training, and motivate your interest in pursuing graduate studies in my group (MSc or PhD).

A background in one or more of the following areas is highly desirable:

  • astronomy & astrophysics
  • stellar/gravitational dynamics
  • astrostatistics and data analysis
  • high-performance computing and numerical simulations

Strong Canadian applicants are encouraged to apply for the following scholarships (along with their application to the graduate program - see above) and list Saint Mary's University in their choice of host university:

  • Canada Graduate Research Scholarship (MSc) ($27,000 for 12 months; deadline December 1)
  • Canada Graduate Research Scholarship (PhD) ($40,000 per year for 3 years; deadline October 17)

Exceptional applicants of any nationality are encouraged to apply for the Durland Scholarships in Graduate Research (along with their application to the graduate program - see above). These university-wide renewable graduate entrance scholarships are meant to attract and retain the best and brightest graduate students to Saint Mary's University. They are valued at $10,000 per year (MSc) and $15,000 per year (PhD).

If you do plan to apply for any of the competitive scholarships above, do contact me well in advance of the deadline to make sure that your proposal is well aligned with the group's research program.

Postdoctoral fellowships

I am always interested in hearing from prospective postdoctoral researchers. Please send me an email including your CV and a brief description of your research interests. At the moment, note that there is no opening for a postdoctoral position in my group, but you may consider the fellowship program below.

Canadians, and international researchers with a PhD from a Canadian institution, are eligible to apply for a Canada Postdoctoral Research Award, worth $70,000 per year for two years. I would be delighted to host a fellow in my group at Saint Mary's and to support your application. The deadline is usually in mid-October, so do get in touch well in advance to prepare your application.

I also welcome enquiries from researchers interested in being hosted in my group for (part of) their international postdoctoral fellowship (e.g. the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions Global Postdoctoral Fellowships).
